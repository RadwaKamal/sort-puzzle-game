Potion Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Potion Sort is a casual puzzle game. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used. The short version: the app itself collects nothing — all game progress stays on your device — but it shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some data of its own to do that.

No account, no backend

Potion Sort has no sign-up, no login, and no server of its own. The app works fully offline except when loading an ad. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email, location, or any other personal information.

What's stored on your device

Your current level, which levels you've unlocked, and your sound/haptics preferences are saved locally on your device only (via Android's standard app storage on the native app, or your browser's local storage on the web version). This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob — a short rewarded video ad when you choose to use a helper (extra undo, extra flask, or skip level), and an occasional interstitial ad between levels. To serve and measure these ads, Google AdMob and its ad partners may collect information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad interaction data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can limit ad personalization through your device's ad settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children's privacy

Potion Sort is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If this changes in the future, ad serving will be configured accordingly (e.g. non-personalized ads) to meet applicable requirements.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Material changes will be reflected here before taking effect.
